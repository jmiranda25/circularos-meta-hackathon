## Suggested copy

**Name:** CircularOS

**Tagline:** Circular Intelligence for Everyone.

**Problem:** Waste identification alone does not tell people what to do next. Decisions depend on contamination, reuse potential, real receiving channels and accountability. An object can look recyclable and still lack a practical destination.

**Solution:** CircularOS combines Muse Spark perception, optional SAM 3.1 grounding, human correction, declared household/business context and a versioned decision engine. It generates explainable next steps and unresolved checks, then exports an auditable plan. Changing the material or available collection channel changes the recommended action.

**Meta models:** Muse Spark 1.3 identifies visible objects and proposes material/condition hypotheses with evidence. SAM 3.1 grounds a selected object with boxes and pixel masks, decoded with Meta's official parser. All live inference is server-side through Meta Model API.

**Technical distinction:** Model output is validated and reviewed before ordinary ready-to-act recommendations. A deterministic policy separates perception from action, prioritizes specialized handling for hazardous/electronic materials, and never invents local service coverage. The export ties original proposals, corrected inputs, context and rule version together.

**Current limitations:** No verified municipal acceptance database, calibrated confidence, measured carbon savings or verification of physical recycling. Business accountability is role-based guidance rather than a deployed enterprise system. Initial hosted access is private until judge access and usage controls are reviewed.
