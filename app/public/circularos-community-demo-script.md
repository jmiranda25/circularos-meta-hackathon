# CircularOS — Updated demo

## From objects to decisions

A bottle, a cap, an old phone. Recognizing them is only the beginning. Circular OS connects visual evidence, human review, local context, and clear actions, helping households and businesses decide what to do next.

## Add photos without starting over

Take or upload a photo, and review it before sending it to Meta. Then photograph another product or add another image. Earlier objects and corrections stay in the assessment, up to eight objects, each linked to its own photo.

## Muse Spark builds the evidence

Muse Spark proposes visible objects, materials, and condition, and transcribes readable labels. Textiles, mixed products, and electronics have distinct categories. These suggestions need review: an image cannot prove fibre composition, weight, commercial value, or acceptance by a recycling centre.

## Highlight the object with SAM

Select Highlight object, and SAM three point one draws a box and mask on the correct photo. Inspect the evidence, correct the classification, and confirm your review. The original model proposal remains in the technical record.

## Context changes the recommendation

Choose household or business, and declare receiving channels you have confirmed. Reuse and composting access also matter. Versioned rules produce reproducible recommendations. Electronics stay on a specialist route. A district name never becomes an invented collection point or a promise of acceptance.

## Guidance people can follow

Larger controls and natural voice guidance make the steps easier to follow. Enable audio preparation, and the next screen waits until its guide is ready. Continue without audio, or press Play to listen. English and Spanish guidance are available.

## A plan you can actually use

The plan explains what to do, why, and what still needs checking. Pending reviews cannot masquerade as completed tasks. Download a readable PDF, print it, or export technical evidence and policy details. A task checkmark is never proof of delivery.

## A free pilot with a social purpose

Our proposed free pilot starts in Lince, Lima, with older adults in mind. The aim is participation and potential income through agreed partners. Delivery, weighing, verified receipt, and recorded compensation must come first. Partners and payments are not yet live. Future business services could support organizations that need accountability.
